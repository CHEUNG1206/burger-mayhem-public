Burger Mayhem - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Burger Mayhem ("the game") is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Data the game stores on your device

Your progress (day, cash, upgrades, settings, whether you bought "Remove Ads") is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad ("watch an ad to double your tips") and an occasional ad between days. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Information about how you interact with ads, and crash / performance data

This is used to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can remove ads and restore the purchase later.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and contains cartoon food-throwing slapstick. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is only shared with Google as described above.
